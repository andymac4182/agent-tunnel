03 / A CLEAR BOUNDARY

A gateway.
Not another agent platform.

Keep your model, orchestration and conversation history where they belong. Agent Uplink focuses on access to local capabilities.

Deliberate accessDevice, service and operation scopes, backed by local configuration.

Honest failure semanticsTransport delivery is not proof of a completed action. Ambiguous effects are not automatically replayed.

An explicit trust modelThe relay terminates encrypted connections and is trusted with service traffic. This is not end-to-end encryption.

04 / BUILDING IN THE OPEN ABOUT OUR LIMITS

Early days.
Solid boundaries.

The authenticated transport and ordered rotation have local verification evidence. Adapter, cluster, platform and release work remain. Agent Uplink is not yet a production-ready release or a generally available hosted service.
